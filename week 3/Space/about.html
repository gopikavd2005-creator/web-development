What is Space?

Space is the vast area beyond Earth and its atmosphere. It contains stars, planets, moons, galaxies and many other objects.

Our Solar System

Our Solar System consists of the Sun and eight planets. Earth is one of these planets and is the only known planet that supports life.

Why is Space Important?

  • It helps us understand the universe.
  • It helps scientists discover new planets.
  • Space exploration improves technology.
  • It helps us learn more about Earth.
